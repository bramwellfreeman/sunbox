Sunbox

Your inbox, at sunrise. Gmail in your terminal, with an AI assistant that sorts your mail, drafts your replies and gives every email a one-press next step.

Sunbox is the email app I use every day. It's fast and keyboard-first, and it runs in the Terminal on a Mac or Linux. Pick the AI assistant you already pay for (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok). It reads your inbox, sorts it into categories that fit your mail, and writes a draft for everything that needs a reply. Nothing is ever sent until you've checked it.

Install it → Source on GitHub

1 · Install

Open Terminal (on a Mac: press ⌘ Space, type “Terminal”, press Return), paste this line and press Return:

curl -fsSL https://github.com/bramwellfreeman/sunbox/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sh

It downloads Sunbox for your computer (Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, or 64-bit Linux), puts it in ~/.local/bin, and sets up your Terminal so the sunbox command works. Then close that Terminal window and open a new one.

Rather download it yourself? Mac download · Linux download. A Mac blocks apps downloaded in the browser until you approve them, so the Terminal line above is the easy way.

2 · Sign in to Gmail

Sunbox is in beta. Google only lets invited testers sign in for now, so ask me for an invite first. With it you'll get the sign-in key used below.
  1. Give Sunbox the sign-in key from your invite:
    sunbox auth setup
  2. Sign in with Google. Your browser opens, so pick your Gmail account. If Google says the app isn't verified, click Advanced → Continue.
    sunbox auth login
  3. Open your inbox:
    sunbox

Your mail goes straight between your computer and Gmail. Everything else (your contacts, templates and settings) stays on your computer.

3 · Find your way around

Sunbox runs on the keyboard, but you can also click: click a row to select it, double-click to open it, and scroll with the wheel. Press ? anytime to see every key.

Got more than one Gmail account? Open ⚙ → Accounts to add another, switch between them, or tick a few to show their inboxes together.

4 · Your AI assistant

Open ⚙ → AI assistant and choose the one you already use. Press Return on it to sign in, then t to check it answers.

ClaudeSign in with your Claude plan (Claude Code), or paste an Anthropic API key
ChatGPTSign in with your ChatGPT plan (Codex CLI), or paste an OpenAI API key
GeminiSign in with Google (Gemini CLI), or paste a Google AI Studio key
GrokSign in with your Grok account (Grok CLI), or paste an xAI API key

The plan-based options need that company's command-line tool installed first (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI or Grok CLI). The API-key options need nothing else.

Then open the ✦ Claude tab (key 7; it keeps that name whichever assistant you pick). The assistant reads your inbox and:

  • Sorts it into categories that fit your mail (Clients, Bills, School…), with the most urgent email first and a one-line reason for each.
  • Drafts a reply to every email that needs one. Press Return to check it, edit anything, then Ctrl S to send. w redrafts it with your instructions (“decline politely”).
  • Adds buttons for emails that need something other than a reply. Press Space to see them: add to Google Calendar, add a TickTick task, open the link the email is about, unsubscribe, archive, label or forward.
  • Files your mail into Gmail labels named after the categories when you press F, so the sorting shows up on your phone too.

Press R to have it read your inbox again.

5 · Updates & extras

Update

Run the install line again. It replaces Sunbox with the newest version and keeps your accounts and settings.

From the command line

Every feature also works as a plain command, which is handy for scripts and AI agents: sunbox inbox, sunbox search "from:jane", sunbox contacts find ross, sunbox claude brief. Add --json for machine-readable output, or run sunbox --help to see them all.

Uninstall

Delete the app, plus its settings and data if you want those gone too:

# Mac rm ~/.local/bin/sunbox rm -rf ~/Library/Application\ Support/sunbox # Linux rm ~/.local/bin/sunbox rm -rf ~/.config/sunbox ~/.local/share/sunbox
